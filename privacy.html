Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who we are

Still Water Wealth is a YouTube channel run by an individual creator. Contact: compoundandcalmhq@gmail.com.

Our internal tool

We use a private publishing tool, "Still Water Wealth Uploader", which connects to the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos and to read basic statistics (such as view counts) for those videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool accesses

Only data belonging to the Still Water Wealth YouTube channel: our own videos, their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and view counts. We do not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

How data is stored

View counts for our own videos are kept in a private spreadsheet on the owner's computer for tracking performance. Authorization tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke this tool's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Viewers

If you watch our videos or contact us by email, we only see the information YouTube or email normally shows (for example, public comments or your email address). We do not collect anything else.
